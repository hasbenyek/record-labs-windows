Record Labs 0.2.0 for Mac

The first Mac version of Record Labs. Two installers:
- **RecordLabs-0.2.0-arm64.dmg**: Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 and newer)
- **RecordLabs-0.2.0-x64.dmg**: Macs with an Intel processor

**Mac**
- Smoother on every Mac: the window is no longer drawn again and again while a song plays, lyrics move at up to 60 frames a second, and the app draws less while it is in the background
- Lighter visuals (Settings > Appearance): turned on by itself on slower Intel Macs, can be turned on or off
- The menu bar holds the profile page: account, music recognition, integrations and every settings page
- The window follows the system's light / dark appearance
- Log in with Google through Chrome, Brave, Edge or Firefox
- Updates install from inside the app

First launch: the app is not signed with an Apple developer certificate, so right-click Record Labs in Applications and choose Open (once).

Windows and Linux stay on 0.1.9; this release has nothing new for them.
