Record Labs 0.1.7

**Linux**
- Google login now finishes on Fedora and other systems where Firefox is the default browser: Record Labs waits for YouTube Music to accept the session before closing the login window
- If login still doesn't finish, the reason is written to ~/.cache/record-labs/google-login.log

**Also in this version (from 0.1.6)**
- Linux: no more stuttering in the first seconds of a song on PipeWire desktops (Fedora, Debian…)
- Lyrics font: choose Display (default) or Classic from the ⋮ menu on the music player and lyrics page
- Right-click a song in Quick picks, Trending and similar lists to open its menu
- The X button on the side Queue panel closes it again
- Fedora: the app is no longer too small at 125% / 166% display scaling
