Record Labs 0.1.8

**Record Labs Connect**
- Works with Record Labs 2.0 for Android: move playback between your computer and your phone in both directions
- Handing playback to another device is faster and no longer stays stuck on the computer
- The devices menu opens and closes with a smooth animation

**Listen Together**
- The connection no longer drops every minute; after a short network hiccup the room reconnects by itself and nothing you sent is lost

**Player and lyrics**
- New "Animated artwork" switch in the ⋯ menu of the music player and the lyrics page
- In portrait windows the lyrics preview in the player sits a little higher, so it is no longer cut off
- Opening the lyrics page no longer shows the title and artwork twice during the animation

**Home and account**
- After signing in with Google, Home refreshes with recommendations from your YouTube Music account
- Linux (Fedora and others): your account name and profile photo now appear after signing in
