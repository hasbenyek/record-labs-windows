Record Labs 0.1.9

**New**
- Recognize song: the new button in the search box listens through your microphone to the music playing around you and finds it on YouTube Music. Every song you recognize is kept in Recognition History in your Library
- Playlists: create, rename and describe them, choose who can see them (private, unlisted or public), upload your own cover, invite friends to add songs, copy the link or delete them. Changes go to your YouTube Music account too
- Playlists Migration: bring playlists over from Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer or YouTube by pasting a link or a list of songs, and take yours to other apps as a song list or a CSV file
- Normalize volume (every song at an even loudness) and Crossfade (1 to 12 seconds) now work, in Settings > Player
- On a Library page the search box searches that page; one tap searches YouTube Music instead
- Albums, artists and podcasts you save with "Save to library" get their own Library pages
- Local music: covers are read from the song files themselves (MP3, FLAC, M4A, Ogg, WMA and more) or from a picture in the same folder
- Lyrics: scroll through them freely, a Sync button brings them back to the song, and the pronunciation of Japanese, Korean, Chinese and other scripts sits under each word
- Home is made more from your own listening (Keep listening, "Because you listened to…", Daily discover and more), and autoplay picks a different queue each time you play the same song

**Windows**
- The installer adds the firewall rules Record Labs Connect needs, so your phone can find the PC
- Opening and saving files (backups, playlist covers, CSV exports) uses the File Explorer window

**Linux**
- Floating lyrics move smoothly: the window is drawn plainly and only when the lyrics change line
- Less processor use while the main window is minimized, and less memory overall
- Song recognition also works with microphones that record only at 48 kHz or in stereo (common on Linux)
